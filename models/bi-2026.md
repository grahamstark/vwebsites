This is a simulation of a possible [Universal Basic Income]() for Scotland. See Painter et. al. (2017).

Quoting Martinelli (2027): 

> "A universal basic income (UBI, used interchangeably with the terms basic income, citizen’s income and unconditional
 basic income) is a regular cash payment, paid periodically

> * To everyone within a defined political community
> * On an individual basis
> * Irrespective of the recipient’s income or wealth (i.e. without means-testing)
> * Without behavioural (labour market) conditions attached
> * Without reference to any prior contributions or work history "

Press the 'run' button to introduce your UBI; results are compared to the actual Scottish tax and benefit system in place Summer 2026. 

The modelled UBI takes the system described in Reed et. al. (2022) as the starting values (Scheme 2, Table 1). (Disclaimer: I am one of the 'et. al.'s in that paper, though I've never actually had the faith, my contributions were very minor, and a different microsimulation model was used). However, Reed's suggested income tax changes are not pre-set.

For more on the model used here, see the notes and references in [the main model front-end](https://sims.virtual-worlds.scot:8444/scotben-2026/).
